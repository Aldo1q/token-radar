TOKEN RADAR

Every protocol without a token, ranked by what its launch would be worth.

Token Radar reads a sealed snapshot of every protocol DefiLlama tracks, keeps the ones that record no token and are not a wrapped, staked or bridged product of one that trades, and values each launch from the snapshot's own figures: its TVL, its seven-day growth, and how long it has run with no token. The multiple comes from the live-token protocols in its own category, so a lending launch is priced like lending, not like a DEX.
