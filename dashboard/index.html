Category benchmarks — the live comps behind every multiple

Each pre-token protocol is priced off the live-token protocols in its own category: the median of their market cap / TVL, shrunk toward the overall median by sample size — (n × category median + 5 × overall median) / (n + 5) — so a thin category's wild median is pulled most of the way to the overall, and a thick category's barely moves. A category with fewer than 3 live comps falls back to the overall median and its rows are badged low confidence. Quartiles give the detail view's sensitivity range. No hard clamp: multiples are only bounded to 0.01x–5.00x.

Coverage audit — where all 758 records went

The board never ranks a wrapped or staked product of a token that already trades, a bridge of wrapped assets, an exchange's balance sheet, or a versioned pool of another protocol: those records are listed under Screened out, each with its reason. A protocol records no token in DefiLlama's data — that is never proof it has none.

Implied value is this radar's own claim, worked out from the snapshot's figures; it is not a prediction that a token is issued, or when. Farm plans are generated from each protocol's own record — its category, chains, TVL and age — with the cost and time of every step stated; gas ranges are ordinary-load estimates, not quotes.
